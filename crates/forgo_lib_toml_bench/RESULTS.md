## Agreement on mutated documents

50958 documents made from the valid toml-test cases: truncated, or with one of 17 characters inserted at a dozen places each. Each is read by forgo_lib_toml and by the three other crates that parse TOML 1.1 into values, and their answers compared: does each accept the document, and when both do, are the values equal?

| Compared with | Same verdict | Same values (both accept) | Only forgo_lib_toml accepts | Only it accepts | …of those, newlines inside an inline table's pair |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| toml | 99.9% | 100% of 20021 | 0 | 46 | 46 |
| toml_edit | 99.9% | 100% of 20021 | 0 | 46 | 46 |
| toml-spanner | 99.9% | 100% of 20021 | 0 | 46 | 46 |

Where they differ, the other crates accept a line break inside a key/value pair of an inline table: a key at the end of one line and its `= value` on the next, for example. TOML 1.1 allows newlines *between* an inline table's pairs, but not within one: its ABNF has `keyval-sep = ws %x3D ws`, and `ws` is spaces and tabs. forgo_lib_toml rejects them. Every difference found is of this kind.
